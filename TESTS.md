# Round 3 tool tests

Each tool was tried in /workspace/ttm-r3 and kept only if it made the videos measurably or visibly better. Cost: $0. Nothing was uploaded.

| Tool | Licence | Verdict | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| iart-ai/explainer-video-skills (isometric rules) | MIT | **Adopted (idea)** | Light isometric extrusions (top face light, side face dark, one fixed light) on the Elevator car and counterweight and on the final scale panel (`Extrude` in Elevator.tsx). Before/after: qa/Elevator/grid_it5.png (v2) vs qa/Elevator/v3/grid_it3.png. |
| iart-ai/motion-design-skills (3 motion layers, parallax) | MIT | **Adopted (idea)** | Kit `ShotLayers` (brand/Depth.tsx): back plate 0.35×, subject 1× with drop shadow, front bokeh 1.4×. Applied to every shot and to the loop frame, so the loop still passes (seam PSNR 48–50 dB). |
| 0xS4N4TG/papercut-explainer (procedural SFX) | MIT (sfx/papercut/LICENSE) | **Adopted** | 20 SFX rendered to WAV with tools/papercut-sfx/render_each.mjs. 8 are used (swish, whip, cut, glint, stamp, count, pop3, micro). With automatic camera-move cues and caption micro-ticks, SFX onset density rose from ≈0.5/s (v2) to about 1.5/s. SFX stay a median 15–16 dB under the voice (qa.py). |
| vincentsch/explainroo (flow comparison) | MIT | **Not adopted as an engine; 2 ideas logged** | It is a full engine with its own renderer and themes, and switching renderers is ruled out. Its flow (script → voice → word check → still/sheet → render → verify) matches ours: we already have the WER check, the review sheets and the QA gate. Ideas for docs/BACKLOG.md: "picture changes at least every 2 s" (ours is ≤3 s beats and a ≤2 s motion hold), the `{shown|spoken}` pronunciation fix, and a second mid-video hook. |
| cclank/lanshu-animated-architecture-diagram | MIT | **Not adopted** | research/diagrams/qr_lanshu.{png,gif}. Its output is landscape with small text and a fixed architecture layout, so it doesn't fit 9:16 explainer beats. |
| d2 v0.9.0 (terrastruct) | MPL-2.0 | **Kept as a tool, not used in v3** | research/diagrams/qr_pipeline.svg and diagrams_ab.png. The output is clean and on-brand, but it is a static diagram and the anti-slideshow rule needs motion. Could become a planning or storyboard aid. |
| lucide icons (lucide-react) | ISC | **Adopted** | `Pill icon=` in the kit. QR "LINK OPENS" (ScanLine) and "NO SCAN" (X) replace the unicode ✓/✕ glyphs. The Elevator uses ArrowUp/Zap pills, a Scale icon and Check/X badges on the ending. |
| @remotion/three (three.js hero shot) | MIT | **Adopted for one hero** | Sky Earth is now a lit low-poly 3D planet with a real day/night terminator. An orthographic, pixel-aligned camera keeps it under the SVG overlays. It needs `--gl=swangle` (angle, swiftshader, egl and vulkan failed with "Error creating WebGL context"). Sky render time is in metrics. |
| slhck/ffmpeg-normalize (EBU R128 two-pass, linear) | MIT | **Adopted as the `master` stage** | research/normalize_ab: v2 single-pass mixes measured −14.9/−15.0/−15.0 LUFS. Two-pass hit −14.0/−14.0/−14.0 LUFS with true peak ≤ −1.3 dBTP and unchanged LRA. That is ~4 s per episode with the video stream copied. Pitfall: it upsampled AAC to 96 kHz, so we now pass `-ar 48000` and qa.py checks for 48 kHz. |
| Qwen3-TTS 0.6B CustomVoice (CPU) | Apache-2.0 (code and weights) | **Not adopted** | voice_ab/compare/*.wav, loudness-matched to −16 LUFS. On the Sky script, all voices had 0% WER (whisper.cpp). Qwen Aiden: 47.3 s / 152 wpm / 10.3 st pitch range. Qwen Ryan: 61.0 s / 118 wpm (too slow for Shorts). Generation was ~2.8–3.5× slower than real time on CPU, versus about 7 s per episode for Kokoro, and Qwen is non-deterministic. Not clearly better, so Kokoro am_michael stays. |
| Kokoro alternates (am_adam, am_fenrir, bm_george) | Apache-2.0 | **Not switched; candidates for a human listen** | am_adam 39.1 s / 184 wpm / 8.1 st; am_fenrir 41.3 s / 174 wpm / 12.7 st (the most expressive by pitch range); am_michael 44.3 s / 163 wpm / 8.6 st; bm_george 44.0 s / 164 wpm / 7.1 st. All had 0% WER. fenrir is the only one with a clear objective edge in prosody range, but nobody has listened to it yet, so it isn't switched. |
| VLM second-opinion review (Gemini or another model) | – | **Skipped** | No Gemini key or other VLM key in the environment, and paid APIs are not allowed. The review was done by the bot from the sheets instead. |
