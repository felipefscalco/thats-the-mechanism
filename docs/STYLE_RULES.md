## Active prose rules

Follow these when writing a script or building a Remotion scene. They are subjective (or not yet measurable) and are injected into the script-writer / scene-builder prompt via `docs/active_rules_prompt.txt`.

1. **Show quantitative comparisons, formulas, and mechanisms as active physical demonstrations (particles, rays, interlocking mechanisms, visual ratios) rather than static bar charts, dashboard tiles, raw formula cards, or disconnected 2D charts replacing physical indicators.**
   - category: `clarity` · severity major · seen in 3 episode(s): Elevator, QR, Sky

2. **Give impacts, climaxes, and transient disruptions a secondary physical response (squash-and-stretch, expanding shockwaves, scale and opacity pulses, continuous burst motion) timed to the matching sound spike.**
   - category: `motion_craft` · severity major · seen in 2 episode(s): ANC, Sky

3. **Vary camera framing: punch in on decisive turns and technical details, break long sequences with macro punch-ins and match-cuts, and mark extreme scale differences with an animated zoom and an explicit magnification indicator.**
   - category: `clarity` · severity major · seen in 2 episode(s): Elevator, Sky

## Candidate gates

Measurable rules that are not yet an automated check. Implement these as `qa.py` gates before leaving them as prose.

- **Strip markdown artifacts, leading dashes, leading underscores, and orphaned hyphen delimiters from caption text before rendering.** (not yet active; 1 episode(s): Elevator)
  - proposed: Assert that caption tokens and formatted display lines do not start with punctuation marks such as hyphens, em-dashes, or ellipses.

- **Break caption lines only at natural clause boundaries, never stranding an article, preposition, or single function word, and never split a hyphenated compound or proper noun across lines or caption chunks.** (active; 2 episode(s): Elevator, QR)
  - proposed: Lint generated caption chunks before render and reject blocks ending a line with a dangling function word or starting a new sentence with one trailing word.

- **Sync caption windows to the spoken phonemes (within about 50 ms), give every spoken word a timed caption token, and flush caption blocks at scene and section cuts so no trailing words bleed into the next beat.** (active; 2 episode(s): Elevator, QR)
  - proposed: Cross-check caption chunk timestamps against section and end-card cut frames and fail if any caption phrase spans across a card transition boundary.

- **Stagger the entrance of paired categorical chips or side-by-side callout tags by at least four frames.** (not yet active; 1 episode(s): ANC)
  - proposed: Check keyframe start times for sibling pill components and assert non-zero stagger offset.

- **Start primary subject motion or a distinct graphical state change within the first few frames (about 12 frames or 500 ms) of the opening shot.** (active; 2 episode(s): QR, Sky)
  - proposed: Verify that the pixel delta between frame 0 and frame 12 exceeds 5% of the central stage bounding box.

- **Transition between sections and out of end cards with crisp element-driven moves or directional whips; never use blur or opacity cross-fades or double-exposure frames.** (not yet active; 1 episode(s): QR)
  - proposed: Enforce section boundaries through a shared world-move transition component and fail QA if any transition frame blends two section canvases or applies blur above threshold.

- **Draw arrows, status markers, and icons as dedicated SVG vector components in one cohesive stroke-and-fill grammar, and never combine an inline text symbol and an icon in the same badge.** (not yet active; 1 episode(s): Elevator)
  - proposed: Reject badge text strings containing directional arrow characters when accompanied by a dedicated icon element.

- **Reserve success, failure, and metric-balance status colors for their semantic meaning; never recolor neutral mechanical components with them.** (not yet active; 1 episode(s): Elevator)
  - proposed: Lint component stroke and fill colors against a whitelist that forbids semantic status tokens on passive machinery or structural containers.

- **Manage the callout lifecycle: introduce a callout only when narration names its component, keep comparison diagrams mounted while narration describes them, suppress background reference markers during metric callouts, clear transient or conflicting badges before the next callout, and remove outdated state badges as soon as the system changes phase.** (not yet active; 1 episode(s): Elevator)
  - proposed: Assert that section comparison overlay components maintain mounted state across all sub-beats of their parent sequence.

- **Animate cause and effect directly: connect every stage of the mechanical chain on screen, show the physical combination step, move objects through intermediate frames instead of teleporting them, deform or redirect trajectories physically on deflection or collision, interpolate progressive changes continuously along active paths, and keep one consistent observer frame.** (active; 3 episode(s): ANC, Elevator, Sky)
  - proposed: Scan animated transform properties across keyframe transitions to ensure the maximum delta between consecutive frames does not exceed fifty percent of the total displacement.

- **Show opposing forces, flows, and rotations as labeled directional vectors that converge on the point of action and agree with the narrated cause and effect; distinguish static gravitational or applied force vectors from motion arrows with distinct styling, keep arrows from opposing actual travel unless labeled as opposing forces, and pair force vectors with unit or concept labels.** (active; 2 episode(s): ANC, Elevator)
  - proposed: Assert that any directional arrow icon within a translating component container matches the sign of the container velocity vector unless an explicit vector label prop is present.

- **Pin persistent headers, progress trackers, and brand metadata to a layer outside camera transforms, backed by an adaptive dark gradient scrim whenever the stage behind them is bright.** (active; 2 episode(s): QR, Sky)
  - proposed: Verify that all persistent UI elements and headers maintain minimum padding within the safe boundary (y >= 100) across all rendered frames.
