## Active prose rules

Follow these when writing a script or building a Remotion scene. They are subjective (or not yet measurable) and are injected into the script-writer / scene-builder prompt via `docs/active_rules_prompt.txt`.

1. **Add secondary physical deformation and burst effects, such as squash-and-stretch and expanding scale or opacity shockwaves, to climactic conflict beats and sudden disruption events, and make particle or wave bursts continuous rather than static starbursts.**
   - category: `motion_craft` · severity major · seen in 2 episode(s): ANC, Sky

2. **Animate the causal interaction between inputs directly on screen, connecting every stage of the chain and showing the physical combination step, and give the climax a bespoke kinetic demonstration with diegetic sound.**
   - category: `concept_wow` · severity blocker · seen in 2 episode(s): ANC, Sky

3. **Demonstrate quantities and mechanisms with active physical behavior, such as moving parts, particles, or interlocking mechanisms, instead of static bar charts, dashboard tiles, 2D stand-in charts, or raw formula cards.**
   - category: `clarity` · severity major · seen in 3 episode(s): Elevator, QR, Sky

4. **Show opposing forces, flows, collisions, cancellations, and additions as explicit directional vectors that converge or meet at the point of action on the recipient, including rotational or balanced-system motion and brake or release tension states, not as stacked graphs or detached equations.**
   - category: `clarity` · severity major · seen in 2 episode(s): ANC, Elevator

## Candidate gates

Measurable rules that are not yet an automated check. Implement these as `qa.py` gates before leaving them as prose.

- **Keep captions, persistent header text, and brand metadata on a dedicated top-level layer outside camera transforms, at full opacity, with an adaptive dark scrim behind metadata when the stage behind it is bright.** (active; 2 episode(s): QR, Sky)
  - proposed: Verify that all persistent UI elements and headers maintain minimum padding within the safe boundary (y >= 100) across all rendered frames.

- **Split caption segments cleanly at title-card and major section cuts so no trailing words bleed into the next beat, and give every spoken word its own timed caption token with none dropped.** (active; 2 episode(s): Elevator, QR)
  - proposed: Cross-check caption chunk timestamps against section and end-card cut frames and fail if any caption phrase spans across a card transition boundary.

- **Strip stray punctuation, leading dashes, orphaned hyphen delimiters, and markdown artifacts from caption text.** (not yet active; 1 episode(s): Elevator)
  - proposed: Assert that caption tokens and formatted display lines do not start with punctuation marks such as hyphens, em-dashes, or ellipses.

- **Never split a hyphenated compound word or hyphenated proper noun across caption lines or caption chunks.** (not yet active; 1 episode(s): QR)
  - proposed: Validate caption word-level tokens against the source script to reject mid-word syllable splits or orphan conjunction attachments.

- **Limit simultaneous floating callout pills and annotation badges on the stage to three, and never crowd more than three into the same focal region.** (not yet active; 1 episode(s): Sky)
  - proposed: Count active badge and pill overlay components rendered simultaneously within the content safe zone and fail if count exceeds 3.

- **Start primary subject motion or dynamic camera framing within the first half-second (about twelve frames) of the opening shot.** (active; 2 episode(s): QR, Sky)
  - proposed: Verify that the pixel delta between frame 0 and frame 12 exceeds 5% of the central stage bounding box.

- **Stagger the entrance of paired categorical chips or side-by-side callout tags by at least four frames.** (not yet active; 1 episode(s): ANC)
  - proposed: Check keyframe start times for sibling pill components and assert non-zero stagger offset.

- **Transition between sections and out of end cards with crisp element-driven moves or directional whips, never with blur or opacity cross-fades that create double-exposure frames.** (not yet active; 1 episode(s): QR)
  - proposed: Enforce section boundaries through a shared world-move transition component and fail QA if any transition frame blends two section canvases or applies blur above threshold.

- **Distinguish directional motion arrows from gravitational or applied force vectors with distinct styling or line patterns when bodies interact, and label every force or physical-quantity vector with its unit or concept text.** (not yet active; 1 episode(s): Elevator)
  - proposed: Require all directional vector components to have a non-empty label prop rendered alongside the vector glyph.

- **Make every directional arrow, torque indicator, and moving part agree with the narrated direction and cause-and-effect; never draw an arrow opposing a body's travel unless it is explicitly labeled as an opposing force.** (active; 1 episode(s): Elevator)
  - proposed: Assert that any directional arrow icon within a translating component container matches the sign of the container velocity vector unless an explicit vector label prop is present.

- **Render arrows and status markers as dedicated SVG vector components, never as inline unicode symbols, and never pair an inline text symbol with an icon inside the same badge.** (not yet active; 1 episode(s): Elevator)
  - proposed: Reject badge text strings containing directional arrow characters when accompanied by a dedicated icon element.

- **Keep the spatial reference frame fixed across states: comparator column positions and the observer's viewpoint must not swap, invert, or move simultaneously with the light source or subject.** (active; 2 episode(s): Elevator, Sky)
  - proposed: Assert that comparative multi-series visualizers preserve constant entity-to-column mapping throughout an entire chapter.

- **Reserve semantic status colors for true success, failure, or balance states, never recolor neutral mechanical parts with them, and retire state badges as soon as the system transitions to its next motion phase.** (not yet active; 1 episode(s): Elevator)
  - proposed: Lint component stroke and fill colors against a whitelist that forbids semantic status tokens on passive machinery or structural containers.

- **Label every comparative chart or multi-bar telemetry graphic with a top-level descriptor, and lock axis labels to fixed layout headers outside the plot area instead of attaching them to moving data bars.** (not yet active; 1 episode(s): Elevator)
  - proposed: Ensure comparative telemetry components enforce a required title or descriptor prop in their component signature.

- **Use one hard-edged, flat-vector stroke-and-fill illustration grammar for all foreground diagrams and icons, and keep backgrounds subordinate, thematically neutral, free of narrative silhouettes, and free of decorative illustrations behind technical diagrams.** (active; 2 episode(s): ANC, Sky)
  - proposed: Ensure background layer behind scientific focal elements maintains low visual complexity and minimum contrast against diagram elements.

- **Dock proof cards beside the diagram with picture-in-picture or split-screen rather than full overlays; when a proof card enters, dim, scale back, or clear the underlying diagram, and fully animate the card out with directional easing before the diagram resumes secondary motion.** (not yet active; 1 episode(s): Sky)
  - proposed: Check that proof cards and modal overlays either pause underlying animated elements or occupy no more than 60% of the stage height.

- **Keep diagrams, comparison states, and cutaways on screen for their full narrated dwell time and until after their animation completes; never unmount a comparison mid-beat while narration still describes it, and never flash a technical cutaway for under 45 frames without secondary motion.** (active; 2 episode(s): Elevator, QR)
  - proposed: Assert that section comparison overlay components maintain mounted state across all sub-beats of their parent sequence.

- **Hold complex formulas and multi-variable comparison cards on screen for at least 2.5 seconds, and pair each equation with an immediate visual ratio rather than raw variable-heavy notation.** (not yet active; 1 episode(s): Sky)
  - proposed: Verify that shots or cards containing mathematical formula markup or multi-bar charts maintain an on-screen duration of at least 75 frames (2.5 s at 30 fps).
