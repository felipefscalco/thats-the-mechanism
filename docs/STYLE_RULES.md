## Active prose rules

Follow these when writing a script or building a Remotion scene. They are subjective (or not yet measurable) and are injected into the script-writer / scene-builder prompt via `docs/active_rules_prompt.txt`.

1. **Always animate the causal interaction between inputs directly on screen—connecting every stage in the signal or mechanical chain and showing the physical combination step—rather than cutting straight from inputs to a labelled result.**
   - category: `clarity` · severity blocker · seen in 1 episode(s): ANC

2. **Build every episode around one unmistakable sensory hero demonstration where visuals and diegetic audio prove the physical effect together in real time, rather than settling for a quiet diagram.**
   - category: `concept_wow` · severity blocker · seen in 1 episode(s): ANC

3. **Always verify that directional force arrows, rotational torque indicators, and moving parts physically agree with the narrated cause-and-effect, and support key mechanical actions with grounded foley rather than generic interface clicks.**
   - category: `clarity` · severity blocker · seen in 1 episode(s): Elevator

4. **Never keep a single framing of one boxed diagram on screen for more than half the runtime; reserve a dedicated full-bleed camera scale shift or visual transformation of at least 2.5 seconds for the script's centerpiece twist.**
   - category: `concept_wow` · severity blocker · seen in 1 episode(s): Elevator

5. **Always depict the promised anomaly, mathematical or mechanical transformation, and failure mode literally on screen from the opening second so a muted viewer grasps the mechanism from visuals alone.**
   - category: `clarity` · severity blocker · seen in 1 episode(s): QR
