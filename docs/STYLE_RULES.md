## Active prose rules

Follow these when writing a script or building a Remotion scene. They are subjective (or not yet measurable) and are injected into the script-writer / scene-builder prompt via `docs/active_rules_prompt.txt`.

1. **Depict interacting forces, causal chains, and physical additions or cancellations as explicit spatial vectors converging on the point of action, animating each combination step on screen rather than cutting from inputs to a result or stacking disconnected equations.**
   - category: `clarity` · severity blocker · seen in 3 episode(s): ANC, Elevator, QR

2. **Build the episode around one unmistakable sensory hero demonstration in which visuals and diegetic audio prove the physical effect together in real time.**
   - category: `concept_wow` · severity blocker · seen in 1 episode(s): ANC

3. **Show quantitative comparisons and ratios through active physical demonstrations or mechanical indicators, pairing equations with immediate visual ratios, rather than static UI bar charts, raw formula cards, or disconnected 2D graphs.**
   - category: `clarity` · severity major · seen in 2 episode(s): Elevator, Sky

4. **Verify that arrows, torque indicators, and moving parts match the narrated cause and effect, and anchor shadows, calipers, and intensity changes to physical geometry so the mechanism reads correctly with sound off.**
   - category: `clarity` · severity blocker · seen in 2 episode(s): Elevator, Sky

## Candidate gates

- **Render captions and persistent chrome on a dedicated top-level track at full opacity, isolated from camera zooms, overlays, and transitions so nothing moves or dims them.** (active; 2 episode(s): QR, Sky)
  - proposed: Verify that all persistent UI elements and headers maintain minimum padding within the safe boundary (y >= 100) across all rendered frames.

- **Reveal captions and stage labels at full target contrast from their first visible frame using position or mask reveals rather than opacity ramps, and end each caption block before any hard scene cut.** (active; 2 episode(s): Elevator, QR)
  - proposed: Cross-check caption chunk timestamps against section and end-card cut frames and fail if any caption phrase spans across a card transition boundary.

- **Never split a hyphenated proper noun or technical term across caption lines or caption chunks.** (not yet active; 1 episode(s): QR)
  - proposed: Validate caption word-level tokens against the source script to reject mid-word syllable splits or orphan conjunction attachments.

- **Break caption text at natural clause boundaries, never strand an article, preposition, or lone short word on a line, and cap burned-in captions at two lines kept clear of the stage hero and navigation deck.** (active; 2 episode(s): Elevator, QR)
  - proposed: Lint generated caption chunks before render and reject blocks ending a line with a dangling function word or starting a new sentence with one trailing word.

- **Sync each caption window to its spoken phonemes within 50 ms, and give every spoken word its own timed caption token with none dropped.** (not yet active; 1 episode(s): QR)
  - proposed: Compare caption display start timestamps against forced-alignment word boundaries and fail if any token offset exceeds 100 milliseconds.

- **Start primary subject motion or a state transform within the first 500 ms (no later than 12 frames) of the opening shot, and introduce the core conflict on the hero subject within the first second.** (active; 2 episode(s): QR, Sky)
  - proposed: Verify that the pixel delta between frame 0 and frame 12 exceeds 5% of the central stage bounding box.

- **Label each force or physical-quantity vector with its unit or concept, style static gravitational or applied forces differently from motion arrows, and never draw an arrow against a body's travel direction unless it is labeled as an opposing force.** (active; 1 episode(s): Elevator)
  - proposed: Assert that any directional arrow icon within a translating component container matches the sign of the container velocity vector unless an explicit vector label prop is present.

- **Sync callout and badge appearance and removal to the narration cue and to the physical state: introduce a component's callout only once it is named, finish it within 0.5 s of the cue, unmount transient or stale badges before the next callout, and keep each demonstration state on screen through the full spoken clause that describes it.** (active; 2 episode(s): Elevator, QR)
  - proposed: Assert that section comparison overlay components maintain mounted state across all sub-beats of their parent sequence.

- **Limit simultaneous floating annotation badges, callout pills, and annotation tags on one stage to at most three, all grouped in one focal region.** (not yet active; 1 episode(s): Sky)
  - proposed: Count active badge and pill overlay components rendered simultaneously within the content safe zone and fail if count exceeds 3.

- **Insert and remove proof cards with directional push or scale easing, dimming or moving the underlying diagram before a card enters, and let overlays animate fully out before the diagram's secondary motion or ray propagation resumes.** (not yet active; 1 episode(s): Sky)
  - proposed: Check that proof cards and modal overlays either pause underlying animated elements or occupy no more than 60% of the stage height.

- **Transition between beats and out of end cards with crisp directional whips or element morphs, never with blur, opacity cross-dissolves, or double-exposure frames, and ease any dismissal back to the underlying diagram along its spatial direction.** (active; 2 episode(s): QR, Sky)

- **Keep background layers subordinate and thematically neutral, draw every foreground element in one hard-edged layered flat-vector grammar with accurate scale and lighting, and clear scene silhouettes when moving to abstract beats; never place decorative illustrations behind technical diagrams.** (active; 2 episode(s): ANC, Sky)

  - proposed: Ensure comparative telemetry components enforce a required title or descriptor prop in their component signature.

  - proposed: Verify that every rendered callout label meets >= 30 px font height, keeps leader endpoints inside y 290..1190, and achieves WCAG >= 4.5:1 contrast on every individual label instance rather than only the 10th percentile.

- **Always enforce uniform single-space word gaps in burned-in captions regardless of active-word highlight styling.** (not yet active; 1 episode(s): FireSprinkler)
  - proposed: Measure horizontal inter-word pixel gaps on every rendered caption frame and fail if any gap deviates by more than 25% from the nominal single-space width.

  - proposed: Compare word-level transcript timestamps for action cues and callout labels against visual state-change frames, failing if sync offset exceeds 0.35 s or if the final proof beat cuts away within 0.8 s of its trigger.

- **Always synchronize callout entrances and exits to their corresponding narration phrases and dismiss superseded labels before the next mechanism state begins.** (not yet active; 1 episode(s): FireSprinkler)
  - proposed: Block the render when label-persistence or label-sync checks miss spoken phrase windows by more than 0.5 s or when consecutive visual beats miss narration word timestamps by more than 0.4 s.

- **Always normalize caption word tokens to single uniform inter-word spacing so uneven horizontal gaps never disrupt caption legibility.** (not yet active; 1 episode(s): FireSprinkler)
  - proposed: Fail the caption gate if any rendered caption line contains consecutive whitespace characters or inter-word pixel gaps exceeding 1.6x the nominal space width.
