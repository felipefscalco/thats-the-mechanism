## Active prose rules

Follow these when writing a script or building a Remotion scene. They are subjective (or not yet measurable) and are injected into the script-writer / scene-builder prompt via `docs/active_rules_prompt.txt`.

1. **Add secondary physical response to transient and climactic events, such as squash-and-stretch, expanding scale or opacity shockwaves, and particle bursts timed to the sound spike.**
   - category: `motion_craft` · severity major · seen in 2 episode(s): ANC, Sky

2. **Show the core mechanism literally from the first second as one unmistakable sensory hero demonstration: animate the causal interaction between every input and the combined step on screen, with diegetic audio proving the effect in real time, rather than cutting from inputs to a labelled result or a quiet diagram.**
   - category: `clarity` · severity blocker · seen in 2 episode(s): ANC, QR

3. **Draw every diagram and foreground icon in one cohesive flat, hard-edged stroke-and-fill vector grammar with accurate scale and lighting, rather than mismatched stock glyphs, soft airbrushed glows, or single-primitive layouts.**
   - category: `style_fidelity` · severity major · seen in 2 episode(s): ANC, Sky

4. **Show quantities, ratios, comparisons, and dynamic mechanisms as animated physical demonstrations with moving parts, particles, or rays, and depict mathematical transformations as physical interlocking mechanisms; never use static bar charts, dashboard bars, raw formula cards, or disconnected 2D charts in place of a direct mechanical indicator.**
   - category: `clarity` · severity major · seen in 3 episode(s): Elevator, QR, Sky

## Candidate gates

Measurable rules that are not yet an automated check. Implement these as `qa.py` gates before leaving them as prose.

- **Strip markdown punctuation, leading underscores, stray dashes, and orphaned hyphen delimiters from caption text before rendering.** (not yet active; 1 episode(s): Elevator)
  - proposed: Assert that caption tokens and formatted display lines do not start with punctuation marks such as hyphens, em-dashes, or ellipses.

- **Break caption lines at natural clause boundaries, never split hyphenated compounds or proper nouns across lines or chunks, and never strand an article, preposition, or single short word alone on a line.** (active; 2 episode(s): Elevator, QR)
  - proposed: Lint generated caption chunks before render and reject blocks ending a line with a dangling function word or starting a new sentence with one trailing word.

- **Time every caption window to the speech it displays within about 50 ms of the corresponding phonemes, and give every spoken word its own timed caption token with no dropped words.** (not yet active; 1 episode(s): QR)
  - proposed: Compare caption display start timestamps against forced-alignment word boundaries and fail if any token offset exceeds 100 milliseconds.

- **Stagger paired chips and side-by-side callout tags by at least 4 frames so they never appear on the same frame.** (not yet active; 1 episode(s): ANC)
  - proposed: Check keyframe start times for sibling pill components and assert non-zero stagger offset.

- **Clear or hide persistent chapter indexes and section titles on the outro and closing callback frames so they match the opening frame.** (not yet active; 1 episode(s): QR)
  - proposed: Verify that the chapter deck component updates its active step index or switches to an outro state when the end-card trigger fires.

- **Start dynamic subject motion, camera movement, or a distinct graphic state change within the first 12 frames (about 500 ms) of the opening shot.** (active; 2 episode(s): QR, Sky)
  - proposed: Verify that the pixel delta between frame 0 and frame 12 exceeds 5% of the central stage bounding box.

- **Reserve semantic status colors for success, failure, or balance states; never recolor neutral mechanical components with them.** (not yet active; 1 episode(s): Elevator)
  - proposed: Lint component stroke and fill colors against a whitelist that forbids semantic status tokens on passive machinery or structural containers.

- **Keep illustrations, comparison diagrams, and resolved state diagrams on screen for a minimum dwell with secondary motion; never unmount a comparison while the narration still describes it, and never flash a cutaway for under 45 frames.** (active; 2 episode(s): Elevator, QR)
  - proposed: Assert that section comparison overlay components maintain mounted state across all sub-beats of their parent sequence.

- **Hold complex formulas and multi-variable comparison cards on screen for at least 2.5 seconds, and pair any equation on screen with an immediate visual ratio.** (not yet active; 1 episode(s): Sky)
  - proposed: Verify that shots or cards containing mathematical formula markup or multi-bar charts maintain an on-screen duration of at least 75 frames (2.5 s at 30 fps).

- **Label comparative charts and meters with a top-level descriptor, fixed axis headers above the plot area, consistent column positions across states, and series labels or meter headers bound directly above or on their own bar, persistently visible while the chart is on screen.** (not yet active; 1 episode(s): Elevator)
  - proposed: Ensure comparative telemetry components enforce a required title or descriptor prop in their component signature.

- **Limit simultaneous floating callouts and annotation pills to three per stage, with one consolidated status badge per component, no duplicate component names, and no mixed text-symbol-plus-icon badges.** (active; 2 episode(s): Elevator, Sky)
  - proposed: Reject badge text strings containing directional arrow characters when accompanied by a dedicated icon element.

- **Animate out or unmount the previous transient badge before the next callout renders, and remove any badge as soon as the physical state it describes has changed.** (not yet active; 1 episode(s): Elevator)
  - proposed: Assert that state label visibility intervals strictly terminate when the opposing velocity keyframe initiates.

- **Show force and motion vectors that physically agree with the narrated cause and effect: arrows follow actual travel direction, opposing forces meet at the point of action, rotation and torque indicators match the moving parts, and balanced or braking systems show tension or vector state.** (active; 2 episode(s): ANC, Elevator)
  - proposed: Assert that any directional arrow icon within a translating component container matches the sign of the container velocity vector unless an explicit vector label prop is present.

- **Distinguish directional motion vectors from static gravitational or applied force vectors with a different style or line pattern, label every force vector with unit or concept text, and draw arrows and status markers as dedicated SVG components rather than inline unicode characters.** (not yet active; 1 episode(s): Elevator)
  - proposed: Scan label strings for raw unicode symbol characters such as arrows or math operators and require dedicated iconography components instead.
